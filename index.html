GUIDEBOOK

How to Play

Cast your line, wait for the lure, then click the pixel-bubbles before they vanish. Each successful click adds 5% catch progress.

Steadiness increases the reaction time of every shake. Luck shifts catch rolls toward rarer fish, while Haste shortens lure time.

Every successful catch can receive a mutation. Some rods force special mutations, and weather can also change what appears.

Use the launchpad in the bottom-right corner to jump between Fishing, Rods, Bestiary, Inventory, Shop, Codes, and this guide.
